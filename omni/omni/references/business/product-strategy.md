# Product Strategy
1. Define the product category.
2. Define the primary user.
3. Define the primary job.
4. Define business outcome.
5. Define differentiation.
6. Define the anti-reference.
7. Define technical constraints.
8. Define scope boundaries.
9. Identify evidence available.
10. Identify what is unknown.
11. Decide what not to build.
12. Use the current stack when reasonable.
13. Align strategy with brand and UX.
14. Keep claims grounded.
15. Note risks explicitly.
