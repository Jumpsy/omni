# Brand Strategy
1. Define 3-5 brand adjectives.
2. Define anti-adjectives.
3. State the primary user.
4. State the user goal.
5. State the business goal.
6. State the emotional objective.
7. Write voice guardrails.
8. Identify product-specific vocabulary.
9. Reject empty hype words.
10. Use samples when real proof is unavailable.
11. Keep tone honest under uncertainty.
12. Align content density to audience expectations.
13. If brand and task conflict, prioritize task clarity.
14. Avoid copying a category leader's tone.
15. Record the chosen brand posture before implementation.
