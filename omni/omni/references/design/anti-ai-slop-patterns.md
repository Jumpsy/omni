# Anti AI Slop Patterns
1. Replace gradient hero wallpaper with product evidence or a plain surface.
2. Replace glowing blobs with a specific illustration or none.
3. Replace glass cards with concrete separation logic.
4. Replace universal 24px radii with a purposeful radius system.
5. Replace repeated centered sections with varied composition.
6. Replace generic claims with product facts.
7. Replace sparkle icons with actual category icons or text.
8. Replace fake logos with real customers or samples labeled sample.
9. Replace fake testimonials with case notes or omit them.
10. Replace fake statistics with measured data or ranges.
11. Replace stock photography that says nothing with useful imagery.
12. Replace repeated icon-heading-paragraph modules with task-based layouts.
13. Replace arbitrary text gradients with legible color.
14. Replace floating nav with stable navigation when possible.
15. Replace empty hero space with content hierarchy.
