# Accessibility Checklist
1. Use semantic HTML.
2. Keep heading order sane.
3. Use landmarks.
4. Ensure focus visibility.
5. Label controls.
6. Announce status changes.
7. Verify contrast.
8. Verify touch target size.
9. Verify keyboard navigation.
10. Verify modal focus management.
11. Verify disabled clarity.
12. Verify zoom behavior.
13. Verify high-content scenarios.
14. Verify reduced motion.
15. Do not rely on automated checks alone.
