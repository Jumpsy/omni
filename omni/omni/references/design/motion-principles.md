# Motion Principles
1. Motion should show causality.
2. Motion should show state change.
3. Motion should show progress.
4. Motion should show hierarchy.
5. Motion should show continuity.
6. Keep common interactions immediate.
7. Respect reduced motion.
8. Avoid layout-jank animation.
9. Avoid entrance-animation overload.
10. Avoid decorative floating motion.
11. Animate only meaningful transitions.
12. Keep timing consistent.
13. Use motion sparingly on dense UIs.
14. Use motion to clarify, not to impress.
15. Test motion in screenshots and interaction.
