# Motion and Interaction
Purpose: make motion communicate change, not decoration.
Workflow: keep common interactions immediate, respect reduced motion, animate only meaningful transitions.
Outputs: motion rules and exceptions.
Failure: excessive entrance animation or independent floating elements.
See [motion](../../references/design/motion-principles.md).
