# Design Research
Purpose: identify the product category, audience, differentiators, constraints, and viable reference direction.
Inputs: brief, repo, screenshots, links, assets, constraints.
Workflow: inspect current product, collect category references, note patterns worth reusing, note anti-references, extract user and business intent, and hand off a concise brief.
Outputs: research summary, reference set, inferred defaults, risks, and recommended direction seeds.
Failure: if you skip real product inspection or confuse surface style with structure.
Delegate: use browser-operator for live pages and images.
Browser: inspect real products, docs, and screenshots.
Checklist: product type, user, goal, goal conflict, assets, anti-reference.
