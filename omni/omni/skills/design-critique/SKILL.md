# Design Critique
Purpose: score interfaces with evidence from rendered screenshots.
Workflow: review product fit, hierarchy, layout, typography, distinctiveness, accessibility, content realism, and interaction.
Outputs: scored critique with evidence and revisions.
Failure: scores without screenshot evidence or inflated praise.
See [critique rubric](../../references/design/design-critique-rubric.md).
