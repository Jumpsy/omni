# Design Critique Rubric
1. Score product fit.
2. Score hierarchy.
3. Score layout and composition.
4. Score typography.
5. Score distinctiveness.
6. Score component quality.
7. Score icon/image direction.
8. Score responsiveness.
9. Score accessibility.
10. Score interaction and motion.
11. Score content realism.
12. Cite screenshot evidence.
13. Use the 100-point rubric.
14. Require at least 88 overall.
15. Reject critical failures.
