# Pre-2023 Design Lineage
1. Study dashboard tools for density and scanability.
2. Study editorial layouts for rhythm and typography.
3. Study enterprise apps for control hierarchy.
4. Borrow structure, not decoration.
5. Preserve reading order.
6. Use asymmetry only when it improves hierarchy.
7. Prefer real content over marketing filler.
8. Treat spacing as meaning, not as filler.
9. Dense apps need smaller type and clearer grouping.
10. Landing pages need narrative flow, not dashboard density.
11. Authentic interfaces show states.
12. Static hero art is not proof of product value.
13. Copy should explain, not merely hype.
14. Avoid trend stacking.
15. If two references conflict, choose the one closer to the product task.
