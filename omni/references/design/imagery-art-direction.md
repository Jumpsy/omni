# Imagery and Asset Direction
1. Create an asset manifest first.
2. Prefer real screenshots for product proof.
3. Prefer authentic photography for trust.
4. Use illustration for abstraction.
5. Use diagrams for relationships.
6. Use data visuals for evidence.
7. Use generated imagery only when coherent.
8. Reject mismatched styles.
9. Inspect every generated image.
10. Reject fake text and bad anatomy.
11. Record dimensions and aspect ratio.
12. Record crop and focal point.
13. Record alt text.
14. Record licensing status.
15. Do not generate images just to fill space.
