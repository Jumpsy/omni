# Unit Economics Formulas
1. Paid CAC = paid spend / paid-attributed new customers.
2. Blended CAC = total acquisition spend / all new customers.
3. Gross-margin-adjusted LTV = ARPA * gross margin / monthly churn.
4. CAC payback months = CAC / monthly gross profit per customer.
5. LTV:CAC = gross-margin-adjusted LTV / CAC.
6. Break-even customers = fixed costs / contribution margin per customer.
7. Runway = cash on hand / net burn.
8. Burn multiple = net burn / net new ARR.
9. Gross margin = (revenue - COGS) / revenue.
10. Contribution margin = revenue - variable costs.
11. Churn must be expressed with a time period.
12. State assumptions explicitly.
13. Show ranges when inputs are uncertain.
14. Never invent missing values.
15. Label estimates as estimates.
