# Growth Metrics
1. Conversion rate = conversions / opportunities.
2. Activation rate = activated users / signups.
3. Retention = returning users / starting cohort.
4. Churn = lost customers / starting customers.
5. ARPU = revenue / active users.
6. MRR = monthly recurring revenue.
7. ARR = MRR * 12.
8. Funnel leakage = drop-off between stages.
9. Cohort retention must include a cohort date.
10. Expansion revenue must be separated from new revenue.
11. Acquisition metrics need source attribution.
12. Distinguish observed from modeled.
13. Do not blend incompatible periods.
14. Show formulas with units.
15. Use honest neutral language when data is missing.
