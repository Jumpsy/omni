# Pricing and Experiments
1. Keep pricing tied to value metric.
2. Show assumptions.
3. Use ranges when evidence is weak.
4. Test one variable at a time when possible.
5. Tie experiment ideas to funnel leakage.
6. Prioritize by impact and confidence.
7. Avoid fabricated growth claims.
8. Avoid fake customer proof.
9. Record expected behavior and stop condition.
10. Separate qualitative and quantitative evidence.
11. Use samples if examples are illustrative only.
12. Document pricing sensitivity.
13. Consider churn impact.
14. Consider margin impact.
15. Consider payback impact.
