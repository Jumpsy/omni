# Anti-Slop Audit
Purpose: find generic or overused patterns and replace them with product-specific choices.
Workflow: inspect hero, sections, cards, gradients, copy, icons, spacing, and imagery; explain each finding and replacement.
Outputs: anti-slop findings and concrete fixes.
Failure: vague claims like "feels generic" without specifics.
See [anti-slop patterns](../../references/design/anti-ai-slop-patterns.md).
