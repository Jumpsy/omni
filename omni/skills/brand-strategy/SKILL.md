# Brand Strategy
Purpose: establish brand adjectives, anti-adjectives, narrative posture, and content tone.
Workflow: infer brand personality, define voice guardrails, align tone to audience, and define content realism rules.
Outputs: brand matrix and copy tone rules.
Failure: if slogans or generic AI language replace product meaning.
