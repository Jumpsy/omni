# Design Lineage
Purpose: translate references into structural principles.
Workflow: separate layout, hierarchy, typography, density, motion, and tone; identify what endures; reject copied decoration.
Outputs: lineage notes, structural borrow list, anti-copy list.
Failure: if references are blended into a generic compromise.
See [pre-2023 lineage](../../references/design/pre-2023-design-lineage.md).
